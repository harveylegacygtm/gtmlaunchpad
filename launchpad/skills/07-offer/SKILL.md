# Offer and Lead Magnet: Build Their Irresistible Pair

Do not summarize this SOP. Build the user's real offer and lead magnet with
them. What they walk away holding: a sellable, irresistible core offer plus a
lead magnet good enough that a cold prospect gives up 30 minutes, split by
campaign type.

Most "my cold email isn't converting" problems are offer problems wearing a copy
mask. Fix the offer first. Work one step at a time. Ask, decide, confirm, move on.

## Step 1: Get their inputs first

Before judging anything, ask:

1. Their core offer: what they sell, the price, and who it is for.
2. The main pain it solves. If they ran the 06-market-research skill, pull the pains and the
   real customer language from that research. If not, ask for the top pain in
   the prospect's own words.
3. The dollar value the offer delivers, for the pricing check.
4. Their niche and a few competitors, plus the red or blue ocean call from the
   06-market-research skill if they already have it.

Wait for answers before you build.

## Step 2: Run the core offer through the gate

Three pass/fail tests. These confirm they have an offer, they do not make it good.

- Real solution to a real pain? Map it to a pain from their research. No match is
  a product looking for a problem.
- Priced right? 10x the value, 1x the price. $100k of value prices at $5k to
  $25k, not $80k.
- Specific enough to picture? "Marketing consulting" fails. "30+ booked calls a
  month within 90 days" passes.

Tell them pass or fail on each. Fix any fail before moving on. No copy saves a
fuzzy or mispriced offer.

## Step 3: Turn the four Value Equation dials

Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort). Two up,
two down. For each dial, write one concrete change for their offer:

- Dream Outcome, up: the biggest specific result, tied to their number.
- Perceived Likelihood, up: proof in their exact niche, specificity, and risk
  reversal (the heavy hitter).
- Time Delay, down: name an early win and lead with it.
- Effort and Sacrifice, down: done-for-you beats done-with-you. Remove their work.

The fast wins are almost always dials 2, 3, and 4. Founders obsess over the
outcome and leave belief, speed, and ease untouched.

## Step 4: Reverse the risk with the Guarantee Ladder

The fastest way to spike belief is to take the risk off the prospect. Four rungs,
ranked by how much they de-risk the buyer and how much it costs you if you miss:

4. Performance / pay-on-results ("you only pay per qualified call booked").
   Removes risk almost entirely, only once your system reliably delivers.
3. Unconditional guarantee (full refund). Rare in services, nuclear when you can
   afford it.
2. Conditional guarantee ("book X calls in 90 days or we work free until we
   do"). Strong default for services, ties your pay to their result.
1. Implied guarantee (the free audit or pilot itself, they see the goods before
   paying a cent). Always available, your lead magnet doing double duty.

Pick one they can survive paying out at scale. Write it in one sentence.

## Step 5: Red ocean or blue ocean

Decide their market state. If the 06-market-research skill already made the red
or blue ocean call, use it. If not, decide it live: search their value prop, scan
competitor outbound, and weigh their ICP plus offer. Page 1 full of the same claim, or
prospects getting 10+ cold emails a week, means red ocean: they need a creative
angle (a specific mechanism, not a generic value prop) and a hard-to-copy custom
magnet. Sparse or generic results mean blue ocean: a direct value prop is the
bait, and they may need no magnet at all.

## Step 6: Build the lead magnet

A lead magnet is a complete solution to one narrow problem that reveals the next
one. Pick one job:

- Reveal a problem (free audit). Strongest in cold outbound, because the
  diagnosis is the qualification.
- Give a sample (free creative, pilot, strategy).
- Do one step of a multi-step process.

Default to a custom magnet in 2026. AI flooded the market with generic PDFs, so
prospects stopped opening them. A bespoke artifact built for their company cuts
through, and AI now lets them mass-produce one per prospect. Run the magnet
through the Value Equation too. Apply the disguise principle: deliver it live ON
the call, not by email, so it qualifies and sells while it serves.

## Step 7: Flex the magnet by campaign type

- Evergreen cash-cow: the magnet does the heavy lifting. One strong, repeatable
  magnet plus a clear value prop, run at scale. Blue ocean may need no magnet.
- Signal: fuse the magnet to the trigger. "Saw the FTC filing, we'll run a
  compliance-gap audit on your exposure." That fusion is why signal campaigns
  convert higher.

Build different magnets per motion, not one universal magnet.

## Step 8: Deliver the artifact

Write out their finished offer: the gate results, the four dials each with one
concrete change, the guarantee in one sentence, the ocean verdict, and the lead
magnet in one sentence per campaign type. Offer to write it to a file. If they
cannot say the magnet in one sentence, it is not real yet.
